I've been building something strange on my workbench.

12 ESP32 boards. A Raspberry Pi. And a sci-fi dashboard that tracks where people are in my house — using nothing but WiFi signals.

Every WiFi packet your router sends bounces off walls, furniture, and bodies. Those bounces carry information: 114 subcarrier measurements, 100 times per second, encoding where you are and how fast you're breathing. It's called Channel State Information (CSI), and the algorithms to decode it have existed in academic papers for years.

What hasn't existed: a complete, open-source system that stitches it all together — firmware, signal processing, localization, vital signs, dashboard. The kind of thing someone could clone and actually deploy.

So I'm building it. Total hardware cost: ~$200. No cameras. No wearables. No cloud.

Blog post coming soon with the technical deep dive. Signal processing nerds, this one's for you.
